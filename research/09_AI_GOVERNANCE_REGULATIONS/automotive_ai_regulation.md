# Automotive AI regulation and safety

## Scope

AI in ADAS/AD, perception, decision-making, vehicle control, embedded systems, validation, explainability, human oversight, model updates, data governance, cybersecurity, failure analysis.

## Standards relationship

- ISO 26262 (functional safety)
- ISO 21448 (SOTIF)
- ISO/SAE 21434 (cybersecurity)
- ISO 24089 (OTA)
- UNECE R155/R156
- ISO 8800 (AI safety, emerging)

## Key points

- AI components in safety-critical paths require safety case and validation evidence.
- SOTIF addresses unknown unsafe scenarios including AI perception failures.
- Cybersecurity applies to AI pipelines and model updates.
- Human oversight required for safety-critical decisions.
