# Regulatory research methodology

For every regulation/standard document:
1. Official title and identifier
2. Issuing authority
3. Jurisdiction
4. Publication and revision dates
5. Current status
6. Applicable dates and transitions
7. Affected stakeholders
8. Applicability to automotive software
9. Applicability to AI systems
10. Technical requirements
11. Documentation requirements
12. Validation and audit requirements
13. Enforcement mechanisms
14. Compliance challenges
15. Industry impact
16. Official source references
17. Research confidence and unresolved questions

Distinguish binding legislation, regulations, standards, voluntary frameworks, guidance, proposed legislation.
