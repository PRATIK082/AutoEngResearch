## Cross-cutting observations

- **Existing tools are strong within a domain but weak across the lifecycle**: no end-to-end digital thread; data moves via file-based, often lossy transformations (W4-001).
- **Compliance tooling documents evidence but does not eliminate the underlying engineering burden**: safety case maintenance and change-impact re-assessment remain costly (W4-011/012).
- **Automation feasibility is generally Medium-High for data movement and validation, Low for safety argumentation and certification**: human approval remains mandatory for safety-relevant changes (all records).
- **AI applicability is highest for drafting, triage, transformation and test generation, lowest for safety-critical sign-off**: hallucination, explainability and certification unresolved (W4-030, W5-017/019/020/025).
- **Open-source tooling is immature/unsupported for production AUTOSAR** but is a named improvement direction (W4-004/025).

## Limitations

- 'Existing solutions' and 'Why insufficient' are as recorded in source reports; some vendor claims may overstate capability (W2/W4 limitations).
- Improvement areas are research directions, not recommendations to build products.
