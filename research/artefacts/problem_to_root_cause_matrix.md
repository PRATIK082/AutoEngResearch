## Cross-cutting root-cause themes (consolidated)

1. **Configuration instead of implementation** pushes all variability into verbose, version-fragile ARXML; tooling and VCS friction compound it (W1-013, W2-001/011/015, W4-002/003/005).
2. **Tool fragmentation is a root cause, not a symptom** - ALM tools store artifacts but do not enforce semantic consistency; cross-tool links are brittle (W1, W2-005, W3-036, W4-001/023/028, W5-011).
3. **Compliance is both genuine safety work and avoidable overhead**; the boundary is organisation-specific and poorly measured (W1-014, W3-025, W4-011/013/014/015/016/026).
4. **MBSE is the proposed cure but is itself blocked** by culture, skills, tooling and governance (W1-009, W1-015, W5-007).
5. **Timing analysis is a safety-relevant unsolved problem** underpinning the zonal/centralised transition (W1-010/018, W2-004/006/013, W3-005/013).
6. **ML/AI introduces new RE categories** (data, ODD, monitors) that classic RE and traceability do not cover (W1-004, W4-030, W5-005/016/019).
7. **Classic AUTOSAR is not going away**; Adaptive has not gained traction as hoped; hybrid is reality (W2 cross-cutting).
8. **Testing is the dominant cost/schedule driver** - up to ~50% of dev cost; invalid failures up to 91% (W3).
9. **Shift-left ambition vs reality gap** - 33% implemented; not testing early enough rising (W3-031).
10. **Talent shortage is structural** - embedded, AUTOSAR, safety, cybersecurity, AI/ML, systems (W5).
11. **AI adoption blocked** by confidentiality, IP, hallucination, certification gaps (W4-030, W5-016/017/018/019/020/025).
