# Global AI governance

## Scope

EU AI Act, India, US (NIST AI RMF), China, UK, OECD, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 23894, emerging standards.

## Summary

- EU AI Act: risk-based framework; high-risk AI systems require conformity assessment, risk management, data governance, transparency, human oversight.
- US: sectoral approach; NIST AI RMF voluntary; executive orders and agency guidance.
- China: algorithm registration, data security, generative AI measures.
- India: emerging framework; advisory guidelines; sectoral regulators.
- UK: pro-innovation, sector-led.
- OECD: AI principles (human-centred, fair, transparent, robust).
- ISO/IEC 42001: AI management system standard.
- ISO/IEC 23894: AI risk management guidance.

## Evidence limitations

- Regulatory status evolving; verify before publication.
