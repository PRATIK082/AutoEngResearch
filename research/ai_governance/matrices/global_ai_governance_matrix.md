# Global AI governance matrix

| Framework | Jurisdiction | Type | Key requirements | Automotive relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EU AI Act | EU | Regulation | Risk-based, high-risk requirements | ADAS/AD, safety-critical AI |
| NIST AI RMF | US | Voluntary | Govern, Map, Measure, Manage | Engineering risk management |
| China AI measures | China | Regulation | Registration, data security | Connected/AI features |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | International | Standard | AI management system | Organisational governance |
| OECD AI principles | International | Principles | Human-centred, robust | Research baseline |
